# Sources and retrieval — emerging companies

| Source | Public material | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) | S-1/F-1 amendments, 424B4 final prospectus, 10-K/Q, 20-F/6-K, 8-K | Filing/registration does not prove listing or completed issuance |
| [Nasdaq IPO calendar](https://www.nasdaq.com/market-activity/ipos) / [NYSE IPO Center](https://www.nyse.com/ipo-center) | Listings and scheduled events | Dates can change; confirm actual trading and final terms |
| [Stock Analysis IPOs](https://stockanalysis.com/ipos/) | Public discovery lists | Verify exchange and issuer sources |
| Issuer IR / customer and supplier disclosures | Contracts, deployments, earnings, capacity and financing | Separate booked revenue, backlog, trials, MOUs and aspirational demand |
| [HKEXnews](https://www.hkexnews.hk/) / [MAYA](https://maya.tase.co.il/) and local exchanges | Prospectuses and local listings | Coverage and disclosure rules vary |
| [Y Combinator companies](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies) / [Techstars](https://www.techstars.com/portfolio) | Startup discovery and founder/company links | Private-company directory, not investment access or verified revenue |
| Startup/company/VC announcements; accessible TechCrunch or sector press | Funding, product launches and market reaction | Promotional evidence needs business verification; paywalled content remains unverified |
| [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/) / [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/) when relevant | Trial registration and regulatory records | Registration is not successful efficacy or product approval |
| Government procurement, patents and sector regulators | Contracts, technical/regulatory milestones | Patents do not prove commercial value; pipeline is not realized sales |

Queries: `"<sector>" IPO prospectus`, `"<company>" first commercial revenue`, `"<company>" cash runway`, `"<company>" customer contract`, `"<company>" offering 424B5`, `"<company>" going concern`, `"<company>" warrants dilution`. Include negative searches and financing risk.

Use all accessible source families that materially address missing evidence; do not require private databases or paid startup datasets. Preserve private-company uncertainty rather than inventing ticker, price, EPS or entry access. See the local skill instructions for the standalone valuation fallback.
