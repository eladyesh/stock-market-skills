# Sources and retrieval — sector flows

| Source | Public route | Appropriate use / caveat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [State Street SPDR](https://www.ssga.com/), [iShares](https://www.ishares.com/), [Vanguard](https://investor.vanguard.com/), [Invesco](https://www.invesco.com/) | Fund pages, NAV, shares outstanding, holdings and reports where available | Historical coverage varies; a single current share count cannot establish a change |
| [ICI flow statistics](https://www.ici.org/research/stats/flows) | Public industry releases | Category definitions may be broad, not an 11-sector feed |
| [ETF.com](https://www.etf.com/) | Public ETF flow articles/tools where accessible | Some history/tools are restricted; preserve actual coverage |
| [ETF Database](https://etfdb.com/) | Free public fund/category/flow pages | Dates and definitions must be checked; not every field is free |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) | Public N-PORT and fund reports | Portfolio snapshots/periodic reports, not a guaranteed daily cash-flow feed |
| [World Gold Council data](https://www.gold.org/goldhub/data) | Available gold ETF flow/holding data | Gold exposure, not all materials-sector stocks |
| [CFTC COT](https://www.cftc.gov/MarketReports/CommitmentsofTraders/index.htm) | Published futures positioning | Position changes, not equity-sector fund flows |
| [FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/) / official macro releases | Rates, credit and macro context | Context, not a substitute for sector-flow observations |
| Exchange/industry-association datasets outside the US | Locally disclosed flows and trading breakdowns | Definitions, currency and cross-border scope can differ |

Queries: `"<sector>" "ETF flows" <period>`, `"<fund ticker>" "shares outstanding" <date>`, `"<issuer>" "net creations"`, `"<sector>" "net redemptions"`. Check both inflows and outflows, and the article's underlying data period rather than publication date alone.

A seed US panel might include XLE (energy), XLK (technology), XLF (financials), XLI (industrials), XLV (health care), XLY (consumer discretionary), XLP (staples), XLU (utilities), XLB (materials), XLRE (real estate), and XLC (communication services). Verify current mandate/classification and holdings at run time. This panel represents those funds, not all money entering the sectors. Expand it where the requested coverage requires and available data supports it.
