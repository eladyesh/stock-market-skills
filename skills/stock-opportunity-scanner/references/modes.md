# Discovery modes

Thresholds are adjustable discovery defaults, not optimized trading rules or proof of quality.

## earnings-gap

Find negative post-results reactions or approximately 10% declines over 1–3 months alongside improving operations. Establish matching dated return windows: last unaffected close before release and first full session close after; optionally 5/20-session follow-through. Match benchmark/sector windows, splits and price type. Do not mix after-hours quotes with closing returns.

Check organic/constant-currency growth, recurring operating profit, cash conversion and guidance versus **previous guidance**. YoY improvement is not a beat; a beat needs dated pre-release consensus on the same basis. Acquisitions and depressed comparisons can inflate growth.

Investigate forward guidance, bookings, margins, working capital, tax benefits, SBC, customer loss, cyclicality and prior valuation. Reject a headline beat masking deteriorating forward economics. Search dated earnings releases and actual market-reaction coverage; open the original sources.

## fund-accumulation

Use named managers or disclose a small diverse panel of active managers with accessible disclosures. Distinguish hedge funds, mutual funds, index managers, family offices and holding companies. Verify each legal filer/CIK; do not guess from brand. Index rebalances do not establish conviction.

Compare corresponding quarter-end 13F tables and amendments. Match identifiers, class and instrument; reconcile splits, mergers, parent/submanager overlap and confidential omissions. Compare quantities first, not price-driven value changes. Keep puts/calls/warrants separate from common shares; their reported amounts do not establish net directional exposure or execution prices.

Record quarter-ends, filing dates, adjusted share change, newly disclosed/increased position and weight in the **disclosed 13F table**, not fund NAV. Describe changes in disclosed holdings, not known purchases at a known time/price or current complete holdings. A previously absent position may have been confidential. Resolve restatement versus additional-holdings amendments before classifying new/exited positions.

13F reports generally arrive within 45 days after quarter-end, omit short stock positions and cover specified securities rather than a complete global portfolio. Verify current rules when needed. Rank magnitude, persistence, independent-manager agreement and concentration alongside manager mandate.

For global managers use 13F for applicable securities and original local substantial-holder filings, fund reports and shareholder letters elsewhere. State actual markets/managers covered. 13D/G and local threshold reports have different timing/purposes; do not pool them into a uniform global purchases measure.

## insider-buying

Use Form 4/4-A or original local director disclosures. Record issuer/owner IDs, role, transaction/filing dates, code, quantity, price, instrument, direct/indirect ownership, post-transaction holdings and notes. Resolve amendments and duplicate economic purchases through joint entities.

Separate P purchases from A awards, M exercises, F withholding/tax, G gifts and S sales. P includes open-market **or private** purchases; confirm notes before saying open-market. A zero-price award is not own-cash buying. Record 10b5-1 information when disclosed. Company buybacks and institutional holdings are different signals; a 10% owner may be a fund rather than management.

Look for several independent officers/directors buying within about 90 days and meaningful increases relative to disclosed stakes. Do not infer personal wealth without evidence. Compare purchase prices with current price, while avoiding unsupported timing claims. For foreign issuers check the current applicable regime; absent Form 4 coverage does not prove no insider buying.

## moat-bottleneck

Map demand -> scarce component/capability -> qualified suppliers -> issuer economics. Seek customer/supplier evidence, qualifications, contracts and results. Examine switching costs, installed base, IP/process expertise, distribution, network effects, capacity lead times and substitution.

Trace the advantage into orders/backlog conversion, recurring revenue, pricing, margins or returns on capital. Quantify segment exposure if available; a small thematic exposure does not transform the entire business. Test competing capacity, redesign, vertical integration, standardization, regulation and demand reversal. Separate a temporary shortage from durable advantage and monetized technology from unproven claims.

## ipo-emerging

Default IPO lookback: 36 months. Label direct listings and de-SPACs. Verify actual listing/trading status and class; an S-1/F-1 or rumor does not mean shares are trading. Read final prospectus, latest reports, cap table and lockup terms.

Check primary/secondary proceeds, float/control, options/RSUs/warrants/convertibles, SBC, burn/runway, unit economics and customer concentration. Use post-financing cash/share basis for EV. Lockup expiry permits sales but does not itself issue shares.

For young public businesses identify observable scaling milestones: paid deployments, repeat orders, production yield, approvals, utilization, retention or gross-profit dollars. Treat management/SPAC forecasts as assumptions. Include financing and execution scenarios; separate pre-revenue/binary regulatory businesses into a speculative group.

For requested private startups, create a separately labeled private-company watchlist. Verify private status, funding and traction; public prices/EPS or a listed ticker may not exist. Do not fabricate a ticker, investability or access to allocations. Link listed beneficiaries only with evidenced material exposure.

## post-dilution

Verify definitive financing terms: primary shares, secondary resale, actual ATM draw, rights, convertibles, warrants or debt exchange. Shelf capacity is not actual issuance; secondary resale does not automatically give company cash.

Record pre/post shares, price, fees, net cash proceeds, use of funds, completion date and current price. Calculate share-count expansion, ownership dilution, theoretical post-money value/share and pro-forma EV. Ownership dilution and value dilution are different because issuance can add cash.

Use completed issuance in the base case. Model unexercised warrants/conversions separately, adding exercise cash/removing debt exactly once. Do not combine a diluted denominator with omitted financing adjustments, or add proceeds already reflected in reported cash. Separate weighted-average EPS shares from valuation shares.

Distinguish growth investment from rescue financing; check runway, covenant pressure, resets, related parties and repeated raises. Trading below offer price does not establish fair value. The bundled dilution calculator handles only plain primary common offerings.

## growth-value

Look for double-digit operating **and per-share** growth, with history and labeled forward scenarios. A starting profile is >=10% revenue CAGR, improving recurring profit/FCF and supportable financing. Separate profitable compounders and turnarounds; negative-to-positive EPS has no conventional meaningful CAGR.

Use sector-appropriate comparisons: P/E for profitable firms; EV/EBIT/FCF where appropriate; EV/sales only with margin economics; P/B alongside ROE for financials; FFO/AFFO for REITs; mid-cycle economics for cyclicals. Do not apply universal P/E or industrial leverage filters.

Separate company guidance, dated consensus and model estimates. Do not invent analyst coverage. Build bear/base/bull revenue, margins, shares and exit multiples when consensus is unavailable; do not require multiple expansion for the thesis.

Forecast revenue, income attributable to common and diluted EPS for each next fiscal year. Include SBC, buybacks, financing and acquisitions. Model common book equity/BVPS only with an explicit equity bridge; distinguish it from market cap, EV and fair value. Assess management execution, capital allocation, incentives, related parties and audit quality. Stress slower growth, margin normalization and more dilution.
