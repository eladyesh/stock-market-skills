# Sources and retrieval — short research

| Source | Free material | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/edgar/search/) / issuer IR | Financial statements, risk/debt notes, offering filings, official responses | Evidence for fundamentals; not a live locate service |
| [FINRA short-interest explainer](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/short-interest) | Definitions and links to public short-interest data | Discrete settlement snapshots; distinguish publication/settlement dates |
| [FINRA data](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/browse-catalog/equity-short-interest) | Available equity short-interest observations | Check coverage, adjustment and date |
| [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/) / exchange short-interest releases | Public symbol/aggregate reports | Exchange/provider history may be limited |
| [FINRA short-sale volume](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/browse-catalog/short-sale-volume-data) | Public off-exchange volume series | Trade activity, not outstanding shorts or comprehensive all-venue flow |
| Public broker borrow indications or user-provided quote | Available current fee/locate data | Account-specific, time-sensitive; absence means unknown |
| Official courts/regulators, auditor reports and issuer statements | Litigation, enforcement and audit facts | Distinguish allegation, investigation, finding and final judgment |
| Public short-seller reports and accessible financial journalism | Leads and arguments | Interested-party claims need corroboration and company response |
| Public option chains and reports | Put activity and positioning context | OI/volume alone do not identify bearish buyers |

Queries: `"<issuer>" guidance cut`, `"<issuer>" receivables revenue recognition`, `"<issuer>" debt covenant maturity`, `"<ticker>" short interest settlement`, `"<issuer>" response short seller`, `"<issuer>" raises guidance`. Searching the strongest contrary case is part of the investigation.

Use dated disclosures and verify current short-selling rules only when the request needs legal/execution details. Free public sources usually cannot establish real-time borrow availability. Never label proxy data as verified borrow terms.
